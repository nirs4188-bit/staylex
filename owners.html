Terms & framework

The shape of our management agreement.

This is the working framework — final terms are tailored to your property type, market, and service tier. Use it as the start of a real conversation.

Management fee

Custom-quoted per property based on scope, location, and revenue tier. We'll share the exact number on your discovery call — not on a public page where it locks every owner into the same bracket.

Term

12-month initial term, then month-to-month. 30-day exit notice once initial term completes. Performance-based early-exit clause built in.

What's included

Listing across all major OTAs, direct booking on staylex.com, dynamic pricing, 24/7 guest comms, cleaning coordination, restocking, smart-lock management, monthly reporting, and damage claims.

Furnishing

Owner-financed via Furnish4Stay or capitalized at signing. Designed for ROI within 4–6 months on most DFW properties. Owner keeps the furniture.

What you pay separately

Cleaning fee (passed to guests), utilities, HOA, insurance, property tax, major repairs over a threshold, and one-time furnishing if not financed.

Insurance & liability

You maintain STR-rated landlord insurance. We require additional-insured status. Damage protection is built into every reservation via OTA programs and our own deposit framework.

Owner reservations

You can block your own dates anytime through the owner portal — no fee for personal stays.

Reporting cadence

Monthly statement (occupancy, ADR, RevPAR, revenue, expenses, net). Quarterly performance review and refresh plan.

Common questions

What owners ask us first.

My property isn't furnished. Do I have to use Furnish4Stay?

No — but most owners do. We've furnished 50+ STRs, and the homes that hit the market with our packages typically book faster and command higher nightly rates. If you'd rather use your own furniture, we'll audit it and tell you honestly what works.

What's the minimum property to make sense for STR?

In DFW, a clean 2BR in a desirable neighborhood with off-street parking can work. We're best at 3–6BR family homes, lake houses, and design-forward 1BR lofts. Anything smaller we'll be honest about the math.

Do you handle properties outside DFW?

Yes — we operate at Lake Whitney (Clifton) and Lake Ray Hubbard (Garland/Rowlett). We'll consider lake and cabin properties anywhere within ~2 hours of Dallas. Outside that radius, we'll refer you to a trusted partner.

What about HOA & short-term rental restrictions?

We screen for STR-friendly HOAs as part of onboarding. If you're still evaluating a property, we can run an HOA / city ordinance check before you close — it's part of our pre-offer due diligence service.

How quickly can I exit if it's not working?

After the initial 12-month term, 30 days written notice. If we miss our revenue projection by a meaningful margin two quarters in a row, you can exit during the initial term with no penalty — just cost recovery on any unamortized furnishing.
