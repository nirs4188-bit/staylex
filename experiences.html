Included & Optional

Stay protection — covered before you arrive.

Every Staylax reservation includes a damage waiver so you can relax. Optional travel protection adds trip-cancellation and medical coverage on top.

Included in every stay

Accidental Damage Waiver

A small fee added to every reservation that waives accidental damage up to $3,000 — in place of a traditional security deposit. Broken wine glass? Stained rug? Covered, no awkward email from us.

  • ✓ Accidental breakage of furnishings & decor
  • ✓ Minor stains & spills on linens, rugs, upholstery
  • ✓ No deposit hold on your card
  • ✗ Does not cover intentional damage, theft, or smoking violations
Optional add-on

Travel Protection

An optional plan from our insurance partner that covers what life throws at trips. Add it at checkout for a small percentage of your reservation total.

  • ✓ Trip cancellation for covered reasons (illness, weather, work)
  • ✓ Trip interruption & emergency travel costs
  • ✓ Medical & baggage protection while traveling
  • ✓ Underwritten by a licensed third-party carrier

Damage waiver covers accidental damage only and is not insurance. Travel Protection is offered through our partner and subject to policy terms. Full terms shown at checkout.
