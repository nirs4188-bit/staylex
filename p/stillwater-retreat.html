Overview

Stillwater Retreat sits on a limestone cliff above Lake Whitney in Clifton, Texas. Three stories, three private porches — each framing the water — with 5 bedrooms that sleep up to 20 guests. A sunset hot tub, gas firepit, and a full game room make it built for memories. Booked direct, with no platform fees.

The space

Three levels of limestone-edge architecture, with three stacked porches that catch every angle of the water — every room in the house opens onto the lake. The lower-level game room is the heart of Stillwater: pool table, arcade (NBA & Pac-Man), skee-ball, shuffleboard, air hockey, foosball, darts, plus a 75" TV, popcorn machine, and a dedicated poker room with minibar. The master suite features French doors, a fireplace, and an oversized lake-facing window — the bed points right at the water, and the master bath's soaking tub looks straight down the lake through a picture window. Outside, the lower patio faces the water: sunset over the dam, a hot tub to unwind, and a gas firepit when the stars come out.

The neighborhood

Set on the cliffs above Lake Whitney near Clifton, Texas — a peaceful lakeside escape with beautiful sunrises and sunsets, easy water access, and room for the whole group to spread out.
