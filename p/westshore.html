Overview

Enjoy a relaxing getaway at this spacious lake access home, perfect for families or groups. The property features 5 comfortable bedrooms and 3 full bathrooms, providing ample space for everyone to unwind. Enjoy outdoor time around the fire pit, or take advantage of the kayaks for a fun day on the water. With generous living areas and easy access to the lake, this home is ideal for both relaxing stays and memorable gatherings.

The space

This single-story home (no stairs) is thoughtfully designed for comfort, space, and group stays, offering generous sleeping arrangements, multiple living areas, and great indoor-outdoor flow. Sleeping Arrangements Bedroom 1 – Primary Suite: King bed, two lounge sofas, 55” Smart TV, and a private full en-suite bathroom. Bedroom 2: King bed. Bedroom 3: King bed. Bedroom 4: Queen bed. Bedroom 5: One queen-over-queen bunk bed and one twin-over-twin bunk bed, ideal for kids or larger groups. Bathrooms • Bathroom 1: Full bathroom en-suite to the primary bedroom • Bathroom 2: Full bathroom • Bathroom 3: Full bathroom Kitchen & Living Areas The fully equipped kitchen includes everything needed to cook and entertain, including pots, pans, silverware, utensils, plates, and more. Meals can be enjoyed at the large dining table, kitchen bar, or cozy breakfast nook. The spacious living room offers comfortable seating and a 55” Smart TV, making it perfect for movie nights, relaxing evenings, or game day. Outdoor Amenities The large backyard features a private hot tub, barbecue grill, and a beautiful fire pit, making it ideal for both daytime relaxation and evening gatherings. Parking & Comfort The driveway accommodates up to five vehicles. The home has been recently remodeled and professionally decorated, providing a fresh, modern feel while maintaining the warmth and comfort of home.

The neighborhood

Nestled in the peaceful Willow Lake area of Wills Point, Texas, this property offers a unique blend of serene lake living and small-town charm. You’ll be staying on a quiet, scenic street surrounded by homes with beautiful views — including private lakefront settings perfect for boating, fishing, or simply soaking in the sunrise over the water right from your deck. ￼ This part of Wills Point feels like a retreat from the hustle and bustle — ideal for guests looking to relax, unwind, and enjoy nature. The community carries a welcoming, friendly atmosphere typical of small Texas towns, with wide open skies, lush greenery, and plenty of room to breathe. ￼ Just a short drive away you’ll find the heart of Wills Point, known locally as the Bluebird Capital of Texas, where you can explore historic downtown streets, local shops, and eateries that capture the spirit of East Texas
