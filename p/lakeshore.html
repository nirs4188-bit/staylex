Overview

Spectacular lakefront estate just 28 mi (45 km) from AT&T Stadium, ideal for World Cup trips with a luxury twist. The neighborhood

Quite little neighborhood with very friendly and a good vibe. Sidewalks are everywhere and you can easily walk around Cars barley pass by the house, most people that drive around are residents You’ll be conveniently located just 36 miles from DFW Airport, 20 miles to downtown Dallas, and 37 miles from the AT&T Stadium. In addition to the many amazing attractions offered in the nearby major cities, Rowlett has plenty to see and do itself. Be wowed by the human oddities at Ripley’s Believe It or Not Museum, 34 miles away in neighboring Grand Prairie. You can also pay a visit to the first ever theme park in the famous Six Flags chain, Six Flags Over Texas, both 51 miles away. There’s no shortage of fun activities to fill your days!
