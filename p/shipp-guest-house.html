Overview

Escape to this beautifully renovated 2-bedroom, 1-bathroom guest house, featuring a full kitchen, a king-size bed, and stunning lake access with an amazing view. Perfect for a retreat, this peaceful haven offers everything you need for a relaxing getaway. This home is attached to the main house but has an independent entrance, backyard, and hot tub. Cozy up with WiFi, hot water, and all the comforts of home, making it the perfect place to unwind.

The space

This cozy single-level guest house offers comfortable sleeping arrangements: – Primary Bedroom: King bed – Bedroom 2: Queen bed Bathrooms: – 1 full bathroom All linens and towels are provided for your stay. (Please note: hot tub towels are not provided.) Kitchen & Dining: The kitchen is fully equipped with pots, pans, silverware, utensils, plates, and more — everything you need to prepare and enjoy meals. The space includes a large dining table, a kitchen bar area, and a cozy breakfast nook. Layout & Location: This guest house is located on the same lot as a larger main home. While attached to the main house, the guest house has its own private entrance and a small independent backyard. There is no interior connection between the guest house and the main home, ensuring complete privacy. Please note: – The larger backyard belongs exclusively to the main home, so we kindly ask that you do not access that area. – The guest house does not offer direct lake access from the property itself, but if you exit the home and walk to the left down the street, you’ll find public lake access just past the neighboring house.

The neighborhood

This home is located in a quiet, charming neighborhood with a welcoming, friendly vibe. Neighbors are kind, and the peaceful atmosphere makes it easy to relax and feel at home. The streets are lined with sidewalks, making it perfect for a stroll or jog. Traffic is minimal — you’ll mostly see residents driving by, which adds to the safe and serene environment. You’ll be conveniently situated just 36 miles from DFW Airport, 20 miles from downtown Dallas, and 37 miles from AT&T Stadium. Beyond the excitement of nearby major cities, Rowlett itself offers plenty to explore. Discover the unusual and fascinating at Ripley’s Believe It or Not Museum, just 34 miles away in Grand Prairie. For thrill-seekers, visit the iconic Six Flags Over Texas — the first in the legendary theme park chain — located 51 miles away. There’s no shortage of fun activities to fill your stay!
