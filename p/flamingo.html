Overview

Spacious 5BR home 14 mi (23 km) from AT&T Stadium, perfect for World Cup stays and family getaways. Enjoy a private pool, cozy living areas, and a fully equipped kitchen. Located near Dallas attractions while offering a quiet, family-friendly setting ideal for relaxing after big event days.

The space

Welcome to Flamingo, a spacious and vibrant retreat perfect for families or groups looking for comfort and flexibility. This two-story home features five bedrooms with a variety of sleeping arrangements. Three of the bedrooms are furnished with king-size beds. One bedroom offers a queen bed along with a single bed, and another is equipped with two bunk beds, making it ideal for kids or larger groups. The property includes three full bathrooms, providing convenience and ease for everyone, especially during busy mornings. Inside, you’ll find generous common areas designed for relaxation, shared meals, or cozy evenings together. Whether you’re visiting for a family getaway, a business trip, or a group vacation, Flamingo offers the space and atmosphere to make you feel right at home. If you have any special requests or need assistance coordinating your stay, feel free to reach out. We’re here to help.

The neighborhood

Flamingo Way is located in a quiet, residential area of Duncanville, Texas, known for its welcoming community and convenient suburban setting. This neighborhood offers a peaceful atmosphere while keeping you close to everything you need for a comfortable stay. Grocery stores like WinCo, Tom Thumb, and Cox Farms Market are just a short drive away, making it easy to stock up on essentials. You’ll also find a variety of dining options nearby, from family-owned restaurants to popular chains. Some local favorites include Roma’s Italian Bistro, offering hearty pastas and pizzas, and Pappadeaux’s for classic seafood dishes with a southern twist. If you’re craving something quick and casual, spots like Jason’s Deli and Chick-fil-A are close at hand. For outdoor fun, Duncanville is home to more than a dozen parks and recreational areas. Waterview Park and Harrington Park offer trails, open green space, and playgrounds perfect for families or a relaxing afternoon. You’ll also find Duncanville Fieldhouse nearby, a popular local spot for sports and events. Despite its peaceful surroundings, the neighborhood offers easy access to Highway 67 and I-20, putting you less than 25 minutes from downtown Dallas. Whether you’re here for business, visiting family, or simply exploring the area, this location provides a comfortable and connected home base.
