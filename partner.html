The shape of our management agreement.

Common questions

What owners ask us first.

My property isn't furnished. Do I have to use Furnish4Stay?

No, but most owners do. We've furnished more than 50 rentals, and homes that launch with our packages tend to book faster and charge more per night. If you'd rather use your own furniture, we'll walk the home and tell you honestly what works and what doesn't.

What's the minimum property to make sense for STR?

In DFW, a tidy two-bedroom in a good neighborhood with off-street parking can work. We do best with three- to six-bedroom family homes, lake houses, and well-designed one-bedroom lofts. Anything smaller and we'll tell you straight whether the numbers add up.

Do you handle properties outside DFW?

Yes. We already operate at Lake Whitney in Clifton and Lake Ray Hubbard in Garland and Rowlett, and we'll look at lake or cabin properties within about two hours of Dallas. Further out, we'll point you to someone we trust.

What about HOA & short-term rental restrictions?

We check the HOA rules and city ordinances during onboarding. If you're still deciding whether to buy, we can run that check before you close as part of our pre-offer review.

How quickly can I exit if it's not working?

After the first 12 months, give us 30 days in writing. And if we badly miss our own revenue projection two quarters in a row, you can leave during that first year with no penalty. You'd only settle any furniture we financed.
